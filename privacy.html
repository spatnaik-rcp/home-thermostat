Privacy policy

Home Thermostat is used only by the owner of the connected Google account to view and control their own thermostat.

  • Thermostat data (temperature, humidity, mode, setpoints) is read on demand and is not stored, sold, or shared.
  • Access credentials are kept only on the owner's own computer.
  • No analytics, advertising, or cookies are used.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Home app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
